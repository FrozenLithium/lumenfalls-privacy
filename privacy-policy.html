About this policy

Lumenfalls (“the game”) is a dark-fantasy action game for Android developed by WMD Games. WMD Corporations is responsible for the game and for this policy. It explains what information the game handles, who receives it, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We do not receive personal information from the game. Lumenfalls has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to share pictures, voice or text. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number, address or precise location, and it does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or files on your device. We run no servers for the game.

The game includes advertising and purchase services from Google and Unity Technologies. These services automatically collect some technical information from the device, as described below. That information goes to those companies, not to us.

The game has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own, and the Unity engine’s analytics and diagnostics services are switched off.

Game progress stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your device: account level, unlocked Wardens and stages, upgrades, currencies (including Lumen Gems), which in-app items you own, settings, and the order references of completed purchases (used only so a purchase is never credited twice). This file stays in the game’s own storage area and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy in your own Google account.

Advertising

The game shows:

  • optional reward videos that you choose to watch, for example to rekindle your lantern after a defeat, reroll or add an upgrade choice, double the embers from a finished night, or receive a small daily gem gift; and
  • an occasional full-screen ad when returning to the village after a finished night (never during play, never during the tutorial, never right after the game starts). Buying “No Ads” removes these permanently.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. No other ad networks are used. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens (rating “T”). To deliver and measure ads, personalise them where you have allowed it, and prevent fraud, Google’s ad software automatically collects:

  • the device’s advertising ID and app set ID;
  • the device’s IP address, which can indicate an approximate (city-level) location;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • app and ad performance information (diagnostics) and basic device information such as model, operating system and language.

This information goes to Google and is covered by Google’s policies: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Each time the game starts, it uses Google’s consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) to check whether a privacy choice is needed where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland). Ads are requested only once that check allows it. Where a choice is required, you can review or change it at any time with Ad privacy choices in the game’s Settings › About page or in the Lantern Market.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (No Ads, the Starter Bundle and Lumen Gem packs) are made through Google Play. The game never sees or stores payment details such as card, UPI or wallet information; Google Play’s terms and privacy policy apply to the payment. When the Lantern Market connects, the game asks Google Play which items your Google account owns, so No Ads and the Starter Bundle are restored after a reinstall or on a new device. Lumen Gems are stored on your device and, like other progress, are not restored if the game’s data is deleted.

The shop uses Unity In-App Purchasing, software by Unity Technologies. When a purchase is started, completed or fails, it may send Unity technical details about that purchase: the product, price and currency, the Google Play order reference and receipt, device model, operating system version, language, the app version and a random installation identifier. It does not send your name or contact details. See Unity’s privacy policy.

Children

Lumenfalls is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us at warismd06@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Data security and retention

We store no game data on our own servers. Information handled by Google and Unity is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and is kept according to their own privacy policies. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you, and we delete them on request.

Your choices

  • The game can be played offline. Ads and the shop need an internet connection; without one, no ads are shown.
  • Reward videos are always optional; the game never starts one on its own.
  • You can uninstall the game or clear its data at any time to delete your progress from the device.
  • Where shown, Ad privacy choices lets you change your ad-privacy choice; your Android settings let you reset the advertising ID or limit ad personalisation.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
